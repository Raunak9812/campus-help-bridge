About the project

Campus Help Bridge

Campus Help Bridge is a student-led resource-sharing platform created by Raunak Yadav. It is designed to make it easier for students to discover, share and exchange useful academic resources across university communities.

What the platform does

Students can browse and share textbooks, class notes, previous-year questions, lab manuals and study guides. Browse filters help students narrow resources by university, semester and subject. The platform also supports community ratings and reviews, in-app conversations, and optional consultation requests between students.

Why it was created

Campus Help Bridge aims to keep useful learning material circulating within student communities, make affordable resources easier to find, and help students connect when they need academic support.
